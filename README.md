# ☕ Loners Café • A Sanctuary for Solitary Souls

A 3D browser sanctuary designed specifically for introverts and solitary thinkers who want a peaceful space to relax, listen to music, watch changing weather, read, and write in their diary.

---

## ✨ Features Implemented

### 1. 🎮 Smooth 3D Gameplay & Controls
* **Desktop:** Smooth **WASD** / Arrow keys to walk, trackpad/mouse look (Pointer Lock API), head-bobbing, and realistic soft footstep sounds.
* **Mobile / Touchscreens:** Left on-screen **Virtual Joystick** for walking + swipe anywhere on the right to look around + touch action buttons.
* **Sitting Animations:** Walk up to the **Window Stool** or **Wingback Armchair** and press `[E]` (or tap "Sit") — the camera smoothly glides down to a cozy seated viewpoint looking out the rainy window. Press `[E]` or `[Space]` to stand up.
* **Carrying & Placing Items:** Walk to the espresso bar, brew a hot drink, hold the steaming cup in your hands while walking, and place it down on any table!

### 2. 🌧️ Dynamic Weather & Outside Environment
Switch the world outside your panoramic window with the top weather dial:
* 🌧️ **Rainy Day:** Fast falling raindrops, water droplet streaks on the glass window pane, moody sky, and soft rain audio.
* ❄️ **Winter Snowfall:** Gentle drifting snowflakes, frosty window corners, cool outdoor lighting.
* 🌅 **Golden Hour:** Warm amber sunlight rays cutting through the window, long cozy indoor shadows.
* 🌌 **Midnight Stars:** Clear starry night sky with a crescent moon and warm interior lamp glow.
* 🌸 **Spring Breeze:** Soft pink sakura petals drifting in the wind.

### 3. 🎶 Retro iPod with Custom MP3 Player
* Authentic click-wheel interface with mechanical click sounds.
* **Upload Your Own Music:** Click *"Load Your Own MP3 Song"* to drop any `.mp3` directly from your computer — plays instantly and 100% privately in your browser without uploading to any external server.
* **Custom Skins:** Choose between *Classic White*, *Stealth Black*, *Bondi Blue*, *Sakura Pink*, and *Vintage Amber*.
* **Generative Lo-Fi:** Comes with built-in procedural ambient/jazz chords and vintage vinyl record crackle.

### 4. 📖 The Loner’s Diary (Persistent & Private)
* Sits on the antique writing desk with a banker's lamp.
* Lined paper notebook with vintage typography and paper-flip sound effects.
* Mood tags (*Peaceful*, *Cozy*, *Reflective*, *Hopeful*, *Quiet Melancholy*).
* **Auto-saved locally** in `localStorage`: your entries remain safe on your device even if you refresh or close the tab.

### 5. 📚 The Bookshelf Library
* Interactive library with curated calming public domain literature:
  * *The Solitary Fortress* (Marcus Aurelius Meditations)
  * *Rainy Night Verses* (Bashō & Quiet Poetry)
  * *The Book of Solitude & Tea* (Kakuzo Okakura)
* Flip through pages with authentic page-turn sound effects.

### 6. ☕ Barista Brew Station
* Choose your bean roast (Dark Roast, Velvet Medium, Golden Blonde).
* Choose your drink (Cinnamon Oat Latte, Spanish Cortado, Double Espresso, Midnight Hot Cocoa).
* Steams and pours a warm cup directly into your hands to carry around the café.

### 7. 👥 Quiet Companionship & Friends Presence
* **Invite a Friend:** Click the top-right companion icon to get a private room code or copyable link (`?room=XYZ`).
* When your friend opens the link, their 3D avatar appears in the café sitting by the window with you.
* **"Quiet Stranger" AI Mode:** If you're alone and want quiet company, click *"Invite a Quiet Stranger"* to spawn a silent visitor reading a book with coffee so you don't feel alone.

## 🚀 Running the Project

```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start local dev server
npm run dev

# Build for production
npm run build
```

Open **`http://localhost:3000`** in your browser!
