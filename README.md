# ☕ lonercafe3d • A 3D Sanctuary for Solitary Souls

A first-person 3D browser sanctuary designed specifically for introverts and solitary thinkers who want a peaceful space to relax, brew artisanal coffee, listen to music, watch dynamic weather, read cozy literature, water plants, and write in their private diary.

---

## ✨ Full Feature Overview

### 1. 👁️ Eye-View Mode & Smooth First-Person Feel
* **Desktop Controls:** Smooth **WASD** / Arrow keys to walk, mouse / trackpad look (Pointer Lock API), head-bobbing, and realistic soft footstep sounds.
* **Seated View Glides:** Walk up to the **Window Stool** or **Wingback Armchair** and press **`[E]`** (or tap "Sit") — the camera smoothly glides down into a cozy seated viewpoint looking out the panoramic window. Press **`[E]`** or **`[Space]`** to stand up.
* **Pick Up & Roam Around:** Carry steaming coffee mugs, books, or a vintage copper watering can in your hands with smooth breathing sway animations as you explore!

### 2. 🌱 Interactive Plants & Watering Can
* Plants placed around the library and café: *Monstera Deliciosa*, *Fiddle Leaf Fig*, *Desk Bonsai*, and *Hanging Pothos*.
* Walk to the library lower shelf to pick up the **Vintage Copper Watering Can** (`[E]`).
* Walk up to any plant to water it: leaves perk up, water splashes, and an ambient sparkle chime plays!

### 3. ☕ 6 Artisanal Coffee Selections & Latte Art
Brew at the Barista station:
* **Honey Lavender Oat Latte** (Heart Latte Art 🤍)
* **Ceremonial Matcha Latte** (Rosetta Leaf Art 🌿)
* **Salted Caramel Macchiato** (Swirl Drizzle 🤎)
* **French Press Dark Roast** (Rich Crema ☕)
* **Vanilla Sweet Cream Cold Brew** (Ice Float 🧊)
* **Midnight Hot Cinnamon Cocoa** (Marshmallow Cloud ☁️)

### 4. ☀️ Dynamic Time of Day & Weather System
Change both the weather and the time of day:
* **Time of Day:** Dawn 🌅, High Noon ☀️, Golden Sunset 🌇, Twilight 🌆, Starry Midnight 🌙
* **Weather:** Rain 🌧️, Snow ❄️, Golden Sun 🌅, Midnight Moon 🌌, Sakura Breeze 🌸
* Indoor lighting, window glass reflections, and outdoor horizons automatically adjust in real time!

### 5. 🎶 Retro iPod & Radio (Upload Your Own MP3s)
* Fully functional retro click-wheel iPod.
* **Upload Custom MP3s:** Click *"Load Your Own MP3 Song"* to drop any `.mp3` directly from your device — private, in-browser playback with zero server requirements.
* **5 Color Skins:** *Classic White*, *Stealth Black*, *Bondi Blue*, *Sakura Pink*, and *Vintage Amber*.
* **Procedural Lo-Fi Generator:** Continuous ambient 9th/13th chord progressions with vintage vinyl crackle.
* **Vintage Counter Radio:** Click the radio on the counter to toggle cozy stations.

### 6. 📖 The Loner’s Diary (Persistent & Private)
* Rested on the antique writing desk with a banker's lamp.
* Lined paper notebook with vintage handwriting typography and page-flip sound effects.
* Mood tags (*Peaceful*, *Cozy*, *Reflective*, *Hopeful*, *Quiet Melancholy*).
* **Auto-saved locally** in `localStorage` so your entries are never lost.

### 7. 📚 The Bookshelf Library
* Browse calming literature:
  * *The Solitary Fortress* (Marcus Aurelius Meditations)
  * *Rainy Night Verses* (Bashō & Quiet Poetry)
  * *The Book of Solitude & Tea* (Kakuzo Okakura)
* Flip through pages with authentic paper sounds.

### 8. 💬 Quiet Chat, Mic Section & Rate Limiting
* **Whisper Chat:** Bottom-left chat drawer to exchange gentle thoughts with friends or the café keeper.
* **Anti-Spam Rate Limit:** Built-in cooldown (1.5s) between messages to keep the space calm and peaceful.
* **Mic & Sound Section:** Toggle voice presence indicator with animated wave dots.

### 9. 📱 Mobile Phone Version & Scannable QR Code
* Dual-touch controls: dynamic virtual joystick on the left, swipe look on the right, and quick action buttons.
* **QR Code Scanner:** Click *"Show QR"* to display a scannable QR code on screen — scan it with your phone's camera to immediately play on mobile!

## 🚀 GitHub Setup & Push Instructions

The repository is initialized locally on branch `main` with the initial commit ready:

1. Create a new empty repository on GitHub named **`lonercafe3d`**:
   👉 [https://github.com/new](https://github.com/new)
   *(Name: `lonercafe3d`, Public or Private, leave "Add README" unchecked)*

2. In your terminal, run:
   ```bash
   git push -u origin main
   ```

3. Your repo will immediately go live at:
   👉 **`https://github.com/PurohitBhagyesh/lonercafe3d`**
